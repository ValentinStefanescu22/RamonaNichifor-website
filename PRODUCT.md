# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

- Final site: Next.js + Tailwind CSS v4 + a lightweight CMS the client edits herself (CMS choice open: Sanity vs Keystatic vs Decap, decided after the design direction is chosen).
- Phase-1 mockups: `mockups/` (Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 + Motion), published as Claude Artifacts for client feedback.

## Users

Visitors arrive mostly on phones (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp shares). The homepage serves three audiences **equally**:

1. **Parents, grandparents and gift-givers** looking at her children's books („Magia suntem noi” series).
2. **Adults looking for personal-development counselling** — sessions online (video) and in person.
3. **Art lovers** interested in her paintings, as originals or prints.

Secondary later: teenage and adult readers (book categories exist but have no titles yet).

## Product Purpose

The personal home of Ramona Nichifor — author, illustrator, painter, entrepreneur and personal-development counsellor. It presents her work, lets people discover her books and art, and gets people in touch for counselling. Success = visitors understand who she is in one screen, find the book / art / counselling path in one tap, and reach an external bookstore or contact her.

## Positioning

One person behind every piece: the same hand writes the stories, paints the illustrations and runs the counselling sessions. The books are „o poveste pentru copii… o șoaptă pentru adulți” — children's stories that carry emotional-development themes adults recognise, which is the bridge to her counselling practice.

## Operating Context

- Content is organised into **Universuri**: one universe per story character (Universul Fluturelui, Universul Buburuzei, …). A universe is a collection: the book plus related objects (bookmark, playing cards, poster…) that do not exist yet.
- Books have three audiences: **copii / adolescenți / adulți**.
- **Artă**: paintings sold as originals or prints (enquiry-based for now).
- **Consiliere pentru dezvoltare personală**: online and in-person sessions; booking is by contact.
- **No online shop yet** — no cart, payment or delivery until her PFA is set up. Books are bought through external stores (eMAG, Amazon, …).
- The client updates content herself through the CMS.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Bilingual: Romanian (default) and English**, with a visible RO/EN switch. Books are planned for more languages later (FR, DE, ES, AR, HI).
- Mobile-first; laptop must be equally considered.
- Contact channels: Instagram, Facebook, email, WhatsApp (handles/addresses not provided yet — placeholders).
- Undecided: counselling city for in-person sessions, domain name, store links, prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Name/brand: **Ramona Nichifor**. Series title: **„Magia suntem noi”**.
- Voice: warm, gentle, lyrical, intimate; speaks to children and adults at once.
- Confirmed lines: „O poveste pentru copii… o șoaptă pentru adulți”, „Povești pentru suflete mici și mari”, „Între rațiune și intuiție. Între cuvânt și imagine. Între ceea ce știm și ceea ce simțim.”
- **Do not use** „Bine ai revenit acasă, puiule!” / "Welcome home, baby".
- Roles line: Autor · Ilustrator · Antreprenor · Consilier pentru dezvoltare personală.
- Colour cues from the client's own material (to recycle): lavender, blush pink, powder blue, sage, cream, deep plum ink, butterfly magenta.

## Product Principles

1. One person, three crafts — every page should make the link between story, art and counselling visible.
2. Gentle, never saccharine: softness through space and light, not ornament overload.
3. The phone is the main stage: one-thumb navigation, fast, legible.
4. Everything editable by her: structure content so a CMS can own it.
5. Honest placeholders: unreleased universes and missing art are shown as „în curând”, never faked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readers include grandparents and parents reading on phones: body text ≥ 16px, strong contrast on watercolor backgrounds, tap targets ≥ 44px, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Romanian diacritics (ă â î ș ț) must render in every typeface.
