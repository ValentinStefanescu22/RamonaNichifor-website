# Concept A · Poiana — homepage mockup

Mode: Persuade (first-time visitor from social, on a phone). Audiences equal: book buyers, counselling clients, art lovers. Scope: hero + short scroll, RO default with EN toggle.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage is the meadow from her own book cover — you step into the illustration instead of reading a brochure about it. Refuses the category default of centered script-font hero over a stock watercolor wallpaper with three icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Sky wash → her meadow painting at the bottom, soft plum ink type set in Fraunces (soft axis) with Alegreya Sans; lavender/blush/powder blue/sage/magenta spark from the butterfly; arched "window" shapes for universes; no cards with borders, no eyebrows.
STORY: Visitor meets Ramona and the series „Magia suntem noi”, sees three paths (Universuri & cărți, Artă, Consiliere) with equal weight, opens the Fluturele book and leaves for eMAG/Amazon or writes to her.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: wordmark + RO|EN + menu top; large two-line serif headline mid-upper third; roles line; primary CTA; her butterfly (real cut-out from the cover, flapping) hovering right of the headline; meadow painting filling the bottom ~35% with parallax. Three path links sit at the meadow's edge.
FORM: User-selected direction A (no seed roll — the user pinned three directions). Signature interaction: the butterfly flies a scroll-driven path and lands on the Universul Fluturelui window.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
