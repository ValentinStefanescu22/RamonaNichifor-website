# Concept C · Universuri — homepage mockup

Mode: Persuade/Experience hybrid (first-time visitor from social, on a phone). Audiences equal: book buyers, counselling clients, art lovers. Scope: hero + short scroll, RO default with EN toggle.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage is a map of her worlds — each story character is a universe you open with a tap, so the business model (universes = collections) is the navigation itself. Refuses the default shop-like product grid.
OWN-WORLD: Dusk-to-dawn sky (plum → lavender → blush → powder blue) with grain and drifting pollen, watercolor orbs each holding a creature, a dotted storybook path, Bricolage Grotesque display + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next text, frosted bottom tab bar and sheets.
STORY: Visitor sees „Magia suntem noi” and four worlds, taps the butterfly world to see what a universe holds (book now, bookmark/cards/poster soon), then reaches books by age, art and counselling from the tab bar or the cards below.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: wordmark + RO|EN top; headline top-left; four orbs zig-zagging down a dotted path filling the screen, the Fluture orb largest and glowing, the others marked „în curând”; bottom tab bar (Universuri, Cărți, Artă, Consiliere). Desktop: headline left, orbs along a sweeping curve to the right.
FORM: User-selected direction C (no seed roll — the user pinned three directions). Signature interaction: tapping an orb morphs it (View Transition) into a universe sheet that can be dragged down to dismiss.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
