# Concept B · Jurnalul — homepage mockup

Mode: Persuade (first-time visitor from social, on a phone). Audiences equal: book buyers, counselling clients, art lovers. Scope: hero + short scroll, RO default with EN toggle.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The homepage is a page from Ramona's studio journal — a person first, then her work. Refuses the default "portrait next to big name + services grid" personal-brand page.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper with grain and a faint ruled/dot grid, ink-plum text, Instrument Serif display with Newsreader text and Caveat for one or two handwritten notes; washi tape in lavender/sage, pressed-flower accents from her watercolor, taped photos with real rotation; chapters instead of sections; hand-drawn ink strokes.
STORY: Visitor meets Ramona (photo + her manifesto lines), understands she writes, paints and counsels, then flips through chapters: Cărți/Universuri, Artă (the mug, details from the cover), Consiliere (a letter card) and contacts her.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: small journal header (name + RO|EN + menu), taped portrait rotated -3° occupying ~55% width top-left with a handwritten note and a self-drawing ink arrow pointing to it; the three manifesto lines in large serif below with watercolor underlines painting in under intuiție / imagine / simțim; roles line; a chapter index strip. Desktop: two-page spread with a gutter shadow.
FORM: User-selected direction B (no seed roll — the user pinned three directions). Signature interaction: ink and watercolor strokes that draw themselves, and a ribbon bookmark that tracks the current chapter.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
